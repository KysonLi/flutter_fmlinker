1. How we collect and use your personal information

We collect and use your personal information only for the purposes described in this Policy. Our products and/or services include functions necessary for online shopping, presenting associated reading, improving our products and/or services, and protecting transaction security. These functions may require us to process the information below. If you do not provide relevant information, you may be unable to use the corresponding function.

1.1 Information you must authorize us to collect and use

Functions necessary for online shopping

  • When you register and log in, you need to provide your mobile phone number and login password. We will send you an SMS verification code to verify your identity. We may also collect information such as your profile photo, nickname, gender, region and personal signature to facilitate contact and communication between users.
  • To help you search for and view information, we may collect information about the device you use to access our products and/or services, including its name, model, identifier, operating system and application version, as well as browser information, so that content can be displayed appropriately.
  • When you search for goods or services, we retain your search history to avoid repeated input or to show products related to your previous searches.
  • When purchasing goods or services, you need to provide at least the recipient's name, delivery address and mobile phone number in the order. The order also records the order number, the goods or service you intend to purchase, the price payable and payment terms. This information constitutes your "order information". We use it for identity verification, transaction confirmation, payment and settlement, delivery, order retrieval, customer service and after-sales service.

Functions necessary for presenting associated reading

When you use the scanning function for an associated reading experience, to protect the security of content products and enable their rights holders to understand transactions and receipt of content products, we require you to log in to your account first. We may also collect information about the device you use to access our products and/or services, including the device name, device model, operating system and application version, as well as browser type. If you refuse to provide this information or authorization, you may be unable to use the value-added associated reading experience or view relevant information. This will not affect Pan-media browsing, search or other services.

Functions necessary for improving our products and/or services

We may use your order information, browsing history and other collected data to show you products or services that may interest you or to suggest what you may be looking for. To provide and improve our services, we may also process other reasonably relevant information, including feedback and suggestions you provide and information generated through your interactions with content product owners and producers.

Functions necessary for protecting transaction security

To enhance system security, detect phishing and protect account security, we assess risks associated with account use based on browsing, order and device information. We also collect device information to analyze the Pan-media system, conduct traffic statistics, investigate potential risks and troubleshoot when you choose to send us information about an abnormality.

1.2 Information you may choose whether to authorize us to collect and use

To improve your experience, the following additional functions may collect and use your personal information. If you refuse to provide the information or authorization, you may be unable to use the corresponding functions, such as code scanning, book sharing, audio and video sharing, and note-taking. This will not affect browsing, search or other services.

  • Location-based personalized recommendations: We collect your location at that time to protect content products and enable their owners to understand transactions and receipt of content products. We collect location at the relevant time only and do not continuously combine location information to determine your whereabouts.
  • Camera-based functions: Used to record videos, take photos and scan codes.
  • Image-upload functions: Used to upload photos, share photo comments and change your profile photo.
  • Voice-based functions: Used to record audio directly through the microphone and add audio comments to books.

The additional functions above may require access to your location, camera, photo album, microphone or address book so that we can collect information necessary for the functions. Your permission authorizes collection and use of the information for those functions. If you disable a permission, we will no longer collect and use the corresponding information and will be unable to provide the related function. Disabling a permission does not affect the processing of personal information.

  1. Where related to national security or national defense security;
  2. Where related to public security, public health or major public interests;
  3. Where related to criminal investigation, prosecution, trial or enforcement of a judgment;
  4. Where necessary to protect the life, property or other significant lawful rights and interests of the personal information subject or another individual, and it is difficult to obtain the subject's consent;
  5. Where the personal information has been made public by the personal information subject;
  6. Where the information is collected from legally disclosed information, such as lawful news reports or government information disclosures;
  7. Where necessary to enter into a contract at your request;
  8. Where necessary to maintain the safe and stable operation of the products and/or services, such as discovering and handling product or service faults;

At your direction, we may obtain your account information (profile photo and nickname) from a third party and link your account with that third-party account so that you can log in to and use our products and/or services through the third-party account. Subject to applicable laws and regulations, we will use personal information obtained from a third party after confirming that its source is lawful.

2. How we use Cookies and similar technologies

2.1 Cookies

To personalize your online experience and make access easier, we store small data files called Cookies on your computer or mobile device. Cookies usually contain identifiers, site names, and numbers and characters. Websites can use Cookies to store data such as your preferences or shopping list.

You may manage or delete Cookies according to your preferences, including clearing all Cookies saved on your computer. Most browsers accept Cookies automatically, but you can change your browser settings to reject them. You can also clear Cookies saved in the application. If you do so, you may need to reset your preferences each time you access Pan-media, and previously recorded information will be deleted, which may affect the security of the services you use.

2.2 Network beacons and similar technologies

In addition to Cookies, we use online beacons and similar technologies on our website. For example, an email we send may contain a link to website content. If you click the link, we may track the click to understand your preferences for products or services and proactively improve the customer service experience.

  1. We use various security measures within reasonable security standards to protect information. For example, we use encryption, anonymization and other measures to protect your personal information.
  2. The Internet is not 100% secure. We will do our best to ensure the security of information you send to us. If our physical, technical or management safeguards are damaged, resulting in unauthorized access, public disclosure, alteration or destruction of information and harm to your lawful rights and interests, we will assume the corresponding legal responsibility.
  3. We take all reasonably practicable measures to ensure that irrelevant personal information is not collected. We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes stated in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

5. How to manage your personal information

You may access and manage your personal information.

5.1 Accessing and correcting your personal information

Unless otherwise provided by laws and regulations, you have the right to access and correct your personal information at any time, including:

  • Account information: To access or modify basic account information, change your password, add a personal signature or resolve issues related to a phone number, log in and use "My – My Settings".

  • Delivery information: Add, change or delete a delivery address at any time through "My – My Settings – Delivery Address".

Please contact us if you need to access or correct other personal information generated through your use of our products and/or services. We will respond in the manner and within the period specified in this Policy.

5.2 Deleting your personal information

You may delete some personal information using the methods listed under "Accessing and correcting your personal information". You may request deletion if:

  • Our processing of personal information violates laws or regulations;
  • We collect or use your personal information without your consent;

  • We terminate the service and its operation.

If we decide to respond to your deletion request, we will also notify parties that received your personal information from us and ask them to delete it promptly, unless otherwise provided by laws or regulations or they have obtained your independent authorization. When you delete information from our services, it may not be immediately removed from backup systems; it will be removed when those backups are updated.
